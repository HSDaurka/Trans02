Our Mission

To deliver dependable transportation solutions that strengthen Canadian businesses and seamlessly connect communities across North America — with professionalism, precision, and pride.

Our Vision

To become Canada's most trusted privately owned transportation company — where every driver, every route, and every delivery reflects our unwavering commitment to excellence.

What We Do

Transportation Solutions

From full truckloads to time-sensitive freight, we have the capacity, equipment, and expertise to move what matters most to your business.

🚛

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated trucks for your full shipments. Maximum efficiency, direct routing, and full load security from origin to destination across Canada and the US.

📦

Less-Than-Truckload (LTL)

Cost-effective freight solutions for smaller loads. Share capacity intelligently without compromising on speed or reliability.

🌎

Cross-Border Freight

Seamless Canada–USA cross-border transportation with full compliance support, customs documentation, and experienced border-crossing drivers.

⚡

Expedited Shipping

When time is critical, we deliver. Our expedited service guarantees your freight moves with top priority and no delays, 24 hours a day.

🏭

Dedicated Fleet Services

Partner with Trans02 for long-term dedicated fleet solutions. Custom capacity planning built around your operational schedule and volume.

📋

Freight Brokerage

Leverage our extensive carrier network to find the right capacity at the right price. We manage the coordination so you can focus on your business.
